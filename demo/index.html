Real 3D GPU Bevel & Raymarching RGB Color Picker

Blender 3D Controls

  • Left Click / Drag: pick color on the box surface & move the picker dot; drag on empty area to orbit the view (grab cursor)
  • Press & hold the pick dot (a quick click stays a normal pick): the middle circle is the current color (the enlarged pick indicator), then a small gap, the inner = saturation ring (a linear ramp like a classic saturation slider: white at 12 o'clock → current color at 6 o'clock → black at 3 o'clock), a wider gap, and the outer = alpha ring (a dotted track = transparent, filled solid up to the current value with a thumb knob at the end). Both rings carry white thumb knobs. The rings unfold after holding ~0.25s (or immediately once you drag) with a quick scale + fade-in, and fold back with a fade-out; the active ring follows the pointer — rotate on a ring to set its value, hovering the alpha ring never touches saturation (and vice versa); release to fold them back
  • SV Triangle (optional toggle, off by default): hold Shift to unfold the saturation triangle from the current color, then drag inside it to lighten, darken, or desaturate the current hue (marker dot shows your position; release Shift to fold it back)
  • Middle / Alt+Left Drag: free 360° viewport orbit (Blender 3D style)
  • Mouse Wheel: smooth viewport zoom (0.2x ~ 2.5x)
  • Double Click: on box = invert color (white ↔ black); on empty = reset view
  • Keyboard: R reset · F front view · B back view · T top view · Arrow keys nudge rotation (5° / step)
